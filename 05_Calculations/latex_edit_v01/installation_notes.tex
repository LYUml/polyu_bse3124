\subsection*{FCU and containment details}
Every FB01--FB09 assembly shares one 13 A FCU upstream of all four socket ways. Each twin SO01--SO03 likewise shares one 13 A FCU; FS01 has its own switched 13 A FCU. Terminate ring/main L/N/CPC in an accessible junction assembly rated for 6/10 mm$^2$ cables. Put the FCU at the tap before the 4 mm$^2$ local spur; its neutral is switched with line, and PE is continuous. The demand basis is 13 A continuous capacity per assembly; added points require recalculation.

Containment banks: lighting LC-A--G (seven circuits, $C_g=0.54$); A: P1/P2 (four ring legs, 0.65); B: P3/P4 (four ring legs, 0.65); C: P5/P6/P8 (three radials, 0.70); P7 alone (1.00); incoming feeder alone (1.00). Co-located plan paths show routing corridors, not a shared enclosure. Provide separate Method B runs with horizontal clear space exceeding twice the largest adjacent overall cable diameter between groups, using EMSD A5(3), Note 2; where enclosure dimensions govern, space the enclosures at least as conservatively and confirm the cable separation. Thermal separation is provided by cable clearance, rather than a partition alone. Each individual circuit's L/N/CPC stays together. Avoid thermal insulation contact. If these conditions cannot be achieved, regroup and resize before installation.
