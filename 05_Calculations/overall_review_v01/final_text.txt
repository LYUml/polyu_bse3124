FCU and containment details
Every FB01–FB09 assembly shares one 13 A FCU upstream of all four socket ways. Each twin
SO01–SO03 likewise shares one 13 A FCU; FS01 has its own switched 13 A FCU. Terminate
ring/main L/N/CPC in an accessible junction assembly rated for 6/10 mm2 cables. Put the FCU
at the tap before the 4 mm2 local spur; its neutral is switched with line, and PE is continuous. The
demand basis is 13 A continuous capacity per assembly; added points require recalculation.
Containment banks: lighting LC-A–G (seven circuits, Cg = 0.54); A: P1/P2 (four ring legs, 0.65);
B: P3/P4 (four ring legs, 0.65); C: P5/P6/P8 (three radials, 0.70); P7 alone (1.00); incoming feeder
alone (1.00). Co-located plan paths show routing corridors, not a shared enclosure. Provide separate
Method B runs with horizontal clear space exceeding twice the largest adjacent overall cable diameter
between groups, using EMSD A5(3), Note 2; where enclosure dimensions govern, space the enclo-
sures at least as conservatively and confirm the cable separation. Thermal separation is provided
by cable clearance, rather than a partition alone. Each individual circuit’s L/N/CPC stays together.
Avoid thermal insulation contact. If these conditions cannot be achieved, regroup and resize before
installation.
Protection and board arrangement
The complete schematic shows C63 four-pole protection at MS-O1, 4-core 35 mm2 plus CPC16, a
100 A four-pole DB isolator, independent L1/L2/L3/N/PE bars and 15 separate 1P+N Type A 30 mA
RCBOs. Every load neutral passes through its own RCBO; none are shared downstream. PE bypasses
all switches. Label circuits/phases and allow one final circuit to be isolated without interrupting the
rest.
Use B-curve final protection compatible with driver inrush and the Task 3 breaking-capacity/energy
limits. Individual RCBOs limit the effect of a residual-current fault to its own circuit; full short-circuit
selectivity requires manufacturer coordination data.
Emergency lighting assumption
Landlord emergency lighting and exit signage use independent essential supplies, separated from
normal lighting under EMSD 6B(4). These feeds bypass tenant normal-lighting switches and RCBO
load sides. Emergency-system loads are therefore outside this office normal-supply DB.
