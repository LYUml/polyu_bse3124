\subsection*{Normal lighting and local controls}
The design uses 92 PIL PLATO 600 MultiPower 3117955 panels on the 600 mm ceiling grid: sixty 22 W, twenty-eight 28 W and four 38 W, totalling 2256 W. Their nominal fluxes are 2843.45, 3555.30 and 4685.55 lm respectively. Separate circuits serve enclosed rooms and distributed main-office zones. Adopt a maintained mean illuminance target of 500 lx. The connected lighting density is 7.301 W/m$^2$ using the adopted 309 m$^2$ office area.

Seven local passive manual switches S-A--S-G control their corresponding circuits. Specify minimum 20AX, LED switching at least 600 W and make/break/inrush compatibility for the selected drivers. S-A/B/C use separate enclosures because their phases differ. No borrowed neutral is allowed. Routes visit each switch;  include 2 m DB rise plus 1.5 m down/1.5 m back at the switch, giving 5 m assumed vertical allowance per lighting radial. Passive controls add no operating power.

\subsection*{Small power and fixed equipment}
Provide nine four-socket floor boxes, three twin wall sockets and fixed connection FS01. Each floor-box/socket assembly is individually 13 A fused. The demand calculation uses a 13 A continuous capacity per assembly; FS01 reserves a further 13 A for fixed equipment. Building central AC is supplied as central plant and not duplicated in this office DB.

P1--P4 are A1 rings with fused floor-box connections. P5/P6/P8 are A2 radials;  P7 is a fixed radial. Both P6 and P8 serve the source PANTRY;  including FB09 and SO01. P8 is a separate socket group for convenient isolation. Kitchen sockets are separated from ordinary office circuits under EMSD6E(3)(a).

\subsection*{Protection and board arrangement}
The complete schematic shows C63 four-pole protection at MS-O1, 4-core 35 mm$^2$ plus CPC16, a 100 A four-pole DB isolator, independent L1/L2/L3/N/PE bars and 15 separate 1P+N TypeA30mA RCBOs. Every load neutral passes through its own RCBO;  none are shared downstream. PE bypasses all switches. Label circuits/phases and allow one final circuit to be isolated without interrupting the rest.

Use B-curve final protection compatible with driver inrush and the Task 3 breaking-capacity/energy limits. Individual RCBOs limit the effect of a residual-current fault to its own circuit; full short-circuit selectivity requires manufacturer coordination data.
\subsection*{Emergency lighting assumption}
Landlord emergency lighting and exit signage use independent essential supplies, separated from normal lighting under EMSD 6B(4). These feeds bypass tenant normal-lighting switches and RCBO load sides. Emergency-system loads are therefore outside this office normal-supply DB.
