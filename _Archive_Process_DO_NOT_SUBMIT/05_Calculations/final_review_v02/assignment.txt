The Hong Kong Polytechnic University
Department of Building Environment and Energy Engineering

BSE3124 Electricity and Buildings2026/27 Semester 1


Assignment


Students should form groups of 3 students. Each group is responsible for the electrical installation design for a building which consists of 10 typical office floors and a ground floor for main entrance lobby and M&E plantrooms. Each typical floor is assumed to have three tenants (Office 1, Office 2 and Office 3). The architectural layout plan for ground floor and two layout plans for typical floor are given (one with false ceiling grid for lighting layout plan and the other one with furniture layout for power layout plan). 

Each group is required to complete the following tasks.

Loading estimation and transformer room location
Estimate the building electrical load using CLP COP215. Propose the transformer room location by drawing the transformer room and main LV switch room on the ground floor layout plan. Present justifications for the proposed plant room location. The total building cooling load is assumed to be 1000 kW. State all the assumptions.

Layout drawings and MCB board schematics
Choose ONE of the office tenants for doing the lighting layout and power layout design. You should assign circuitories to all lighting units, socket outlets, floor outlet boxes, fused spur units and any other fixed electrical equipment. You should also draw the MCB board schematics for the distribution board(s) of the office. Describe the design considerations of circuit arrangement and protective device selection for small power and lighting layout. State all the assumptions.

Maximum current demand calculation and cable sizing
Calculate the maximum current demand and the corresponding cable size for the incoming circuit to the distribution board of the chosen office tenant and outgoing circuits as well. State all the assumptions.

Each group should finish the above three tasks by completing the answer sheets and finishing the layout drawings.
