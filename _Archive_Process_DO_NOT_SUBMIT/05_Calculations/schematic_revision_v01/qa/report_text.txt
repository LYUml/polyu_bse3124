FCU and containment details
Every FB01–FB09 assembly shares one 13 A FCU upstream of all four socket ways. Each twin
SO01–SO03 likewise shares one 13 A FCU; FS01 has its own switched 13 A FCU. Ring/main
L/N/CPC connections must terminate in an accessible junction assembly rated for 6/10 mm2 ca-
bles. At each connection, the FCU is positioned at the tap before the 4 mm2 local spur, with its
neutral switched together with the line and its PE kept continuous. With 13 A continuous capacity
per assembly as the demand basis, any added points require recalculation.
To maintain the adopted grouping factors, the containment is arranged in the following banks: light-
ing LC-A–G (seven circuits, Cg = 0.54); A: P1/P2 (four ring legs, 0.65); B: P3/P4 (four ring legs,
0.65); C: P5/P6/P8 (three radials, 0.70); P7 alone (1.00); incoming feeder alone (1.00). Co-located
plan paths show routing corridors, not a shared enclosure. Accordingly, separate Method B runs must
provide horizontal clear space between groups exceeding twice the largest adjacent overall cable di-
ameter, in accordance with EMSD A5(3), Note 2; where enclosure dimensions govern, the enclosures
must be spaced at least as conservatively and the cable separation must be confirmed. Thermal sep-
aration is provided by cable clearance, rather than a partition alone. Within each circuit, L/N/CPC
stays together. Additionally, contact with thermal insulation must be avoided. If these conditions
cannot be achieved, the circuits must be regrouped and resized before installation.
Protection and board arrangement
As shown in the complete schematic, the arrangement includes C63 four-pole protection at MS-O1,
4-core 35 mm2 plus CPC16, a 100 A four-pole DB isolator, independent L1/L2/L3/N/PE bars and 15
separate 1P+N Type A 30 mA RCBOs. Every load neutral passes through its own RCBO; none are
shared downstream. Meanwhile, PE bypasses all switches. Additionally, the circuits and phases are
to be labelled, and the arrangement must allow each final circuit to be isolated without interrupting
the others.
Device selection must ensure that the proposed B-curve final protection is compatible with driver
inrush and the breaking-capacity/energy limits established in Task 3. Individual RCBOs limit the
effect of a residual-current fault to its own circuit; however, full short-circuit selectivity requires
manufacturer coordination data.
