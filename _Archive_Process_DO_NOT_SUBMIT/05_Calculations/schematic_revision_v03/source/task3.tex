\subsection*{Incoming design conditions}
The selected tenant is first-floor Office 1. MS-O1 is a 4P4D ComPacT NSX100H TM63D MCCB immediately before the CLP meter, providing isolation and 63 A protection. The 4C35 mm$^2$ PVC copper feeder has CPC16 mm$^2$. Its 28.5119 m plan route plus 3 m vertical/meter-tail allowance gives 31.5119 m from MS-O1 through the meter to DB-O1. Assumed Method B, 40\,$^\circ$C, separate containment and 15--33\% third harmonic give $I_z=99(0.87)(1)(0.86)=74.0718$ A; $48.6<63<74.0718$. The 25 mm$^2$ alternative gives only 59.856 A. The full-size neutral and its loading limit are retained.

The phase-neutral feeder bound uses $(2/\sqrt3)(1.10)=1.270170592$ mV/A/m: 1.945239 V at the 48.600 A design-demand/neutral-current envelope, and 2.521606 V in the separate 63 A stress check. The upstream drop is calculated from the stated busway model below; the previous arbitrary 2.2 V allowance is superseded.

\subsection*{All outgoing cables}
All outgoing cable sizes refer to PVC copper L/N/CPC conductors. Lighting grouped separately has $I_z=24(0.87)(0.54)=11.2752$ A for seven 2.5 mm$^2$ circuits;  all $I_b\le 10\le I_z$. Lighting lengths include 5 m DB/switch vertical allowance.

Each uniform ring bank counts as four current-carrying legs, giving $I_z=41(0.87)(0.65)=23.1855$ A per 6 mm$^2$ leg, above the standard 20 A minimum. With 13 A per point, the ring-network solution gives maximum leg currents 14.658--15.203 A, below $I_z$. However, the radial criterion $I_n\le I_z$ must not be applied to just one ring leg. Continuity and fused-point restrictions are essential.

Meanwhile, the 10 mm$^2$ A2 radial cables have $I_z=57(0.87)(0.70)=34.713$ A;  P7's separate 4 mm$^2$ radial has $I_z=32(0.87)=27.84$ A. In determining route lengths, the power radials include a 2 m vertical allowance, while the rings include 2 m at each DB end. Additionally, each local point spur adds 0.5 m of 4 mm$^2$ cable.

\subsection*{Complete voltage drop and fault checks}
The origin is the customer main incomer outgoing terminals. Adopt a maximum 28 m developed MSB-to-F1 busway route (design allowance, not surveyed height), with phase fundamental current 1374.458 A and neutral fundamental current at most 10\% of that value. KTC2500 has hot $Z=0.021$ m$\Omega$/m. Allow 10\% impedance tolerance and 0.05 V for internal links/connections: $\Delta V_{up}=1374.458(1.10)(0.000021)(28)(1.10)+0.05=1.027900$ V. Meter tails are already included in the feeder allowance. The distribution segment is $(1.027900+1.945239)/220=1.3514\%<1.5\%$. Worst final drop is $1.936871/220=0.8804\%<2.5\%$. These classroom allocations are checked at design demand; the separate 63 A feeder stress check remains below the 4\% total limit. Harmonic voltage distortion is outside this fundamental-frequency calculation; the assumed phase balance must be maintained.

For minimum earth-fault current, adopt TN-S and an origin external-loop acceptance limit of 0.18 $\Omega$; this is not a measured value. The busway hot phase--PE resistance/reactance are 0.282/0.170 m$\Omega$/m (Canalis table, printed p.143). A conservative $R+X$ envelope gives $(0.282+0.170)(28)(1.1)/1000+0.002=0.015922$ $\Omega$, including connections. Hence $Z_e$ at MS-O1 is 0.195922 $\Omega$, and adding the feeder 0.064129 $\Omega$ gives 0.260051 $\Omega$ at DB-O1. Final-circuit checks retain hot conductor paths, minimum voltage factor 0.95 and B10/B32 reference limits of 4.17/1.31 $\Omega$. Uniform ring paths use the conservative quarter-circumference bound.

\subsection*{Overload and total clearing energy}
CPC thermal withstand is checked using $k=115$ for bunched PVC copper CPCs initially at 70\,$^\circ$C (11(2)(c)), with total $I^2t\le(kS)^2$ required. The incoming NSX100H is rated 70 kA; final RCBOs have 10 kA standalone ratings plus the documented NSX100H backup combination below. B-curve devices must limit class 3-equivalent total energy to at most 70,000 A$^2$s for B10 and 90,000 for B32. Accordingly, the selected RCBOs must have manufacturer total-clearing-energy data that meet these limits. Corresponding CPC withstands are 82,656.25 (2.5 mm$^2$), 211,600 (4), 476,100 (6) and 1,322,500 (10), each above the required device bound.

Incoming NSX100H TM63D total energy must be at most 1,000,000 A$^2$s over the relevant fault range, below CPC16 withstand 3,385,600. The 4 mm$^2$ local spurs are also short-circuit protected by their upstream B32/B10 RCBOs: use the conservative 90,000 A$^2$s RCBO bound, below their 211,600 A$^2$s withstand. This does not require treating the fuse's typical pre-arcing value as total clearing energy. The FCU still provides the stated point overload protection and must meet its 6 kA interruption duty.

For WH01, $I_b=1400/220=6.364<10<27.84$ A, and the manufacturer specifies 10 A protection. Its 10 A FCU gives $I_2=1.9(10)=19<28.2576$ A for the local spur. The assumed 0.5 m local allowance exceeds the 0.448 m drawn connection. The appliance assembly has an accessible earthed socket and double-pole isolation with at least 3 mm contact separation, following manual section 10.4.

Under socket fuse overload conditions, $I_2=1.9(13)=24.7$ A. Spur $I_z=32(0.87)(0.70)=19.488$ A gives $1.45I_z=28.2576>24.7$ A. Overload suitability of P1--P4 is assessed using the standard A1 arrangement in EMSD 6C, 6E and Table 6(1), Note 1(i): 32 A protection, copper size at least 2.5 mm$^2$, corrected per-leg capacity at least 20 A, served area at most 100 m$^2$, intact L/N/CPC rings and evenly distributed outlets. On this basis, the adopted 6 mm$^2$ rings meet these conditions. Exactly two individually 13 A fused connections are allowed per ring; no additional unfused taps are permitted. Here, the leg-current calculation checks load distribution; the A1 standard arrangement governs ring overload protection. The actual point-fault calculation gives a maximum 5.147 kA at FB09, below the 6 kA fuse interruption rating; it includes incoming and branch resistance up to the FCU, without credit for the downstream spur. However, its typical pre-arcing 870 A$^2$s is \emph{not} total clearing energy. Finally, the design assumes an FCU ambient temperature of at most 35\,$^\circ$C and a board apparatus ambient temperature of at most 30\,$^\circ$C; a containment temperature of 40\,$^\circ$C remains the conservative cable condition.
