TM-D Thermal-Magnetic Trip Unit for 3P and 4P Circuit
Breakers from 400 A to 630 A
Introduction
TM-D thermal-magnetic trip units for 3P/4P circuit breakers from 400 A to 630 A
are built-in trip units.
They are designed to protect conductors in commercial and industrial electrical
distribution.
The trip unit exists in two configurations:
•
3P, 3D
•
4P, 4D
