Zone Selective Interlocking (ZSI)
The ZSI characteristics and external wiring of the zone selective interlocking
function, are described specifically, page 131.
If ZSI IN is not set to 1 (open circuit between Z3 and Z4 terminals), the maximum
breaking time is 0.08 s regardless of the tsd setting value.
When ZSI IN is set to 1 and connected to the ZSI OUTof a downstream device (or
when the ZSI function is not used and there is a jumper between the Z3 and Z4
terminals), the tsd time delay is used.
The Isd threshold activates ZSI OUT (Z1 and Z2 terminals).
NOTE: MasterPacT MTZ circuit breakers are delivered with a jumper installed
between Z3 and Z4.
