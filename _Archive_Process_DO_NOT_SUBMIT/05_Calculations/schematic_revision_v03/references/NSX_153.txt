TM-D Thermal-Magnetic Trip Unit for 1P and 2P Circuit
Breakers
Introduction
The TM-D thermal-magnetic trip unit for 1P/2P circuit breakers up to 160 A are
built-in trip units.
They are designed for AC and DC general-purpose applications.
The TM-D built-in 1P/2P trip units provide:
•
fixed thermal threshold
•
fixed magnetic pickup
