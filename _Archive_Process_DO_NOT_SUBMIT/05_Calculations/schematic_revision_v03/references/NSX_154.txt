TM-D Thermal-Magnetic Trip Unit for 1P Circuit Breakers
250 A
Introduction
TM-D thermal-magnetic trip units for 1P circuit breakers 250 A are built-in trip
units.
They are designed for AC applications.
TM-D built-in 1P trip units provide:
•
fixed thermal threshold
•
fixed magnetic pickup
Setting the Thermal Protection
The thermal protection pickup Ir cannot be adjusted and equals the value shown
below:
